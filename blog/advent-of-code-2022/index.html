Advent of Code 2022

Andreas Røssland

November 30, 2022

It’s happening! Finally December is here again, and a new Advent of Code begins. This year I’m changing it up slightly, and in addition to solving them in Go, I’m solving some if them in TypeScript.

To go for the leaderboard or not?

The timing for Advent of Code is a bit unfortunate in my time zone, for night owls such as myself. I tried waking up 05:45 every day a few years ago, but this lead to sleep deprivation and “AoC”-burnout.

Instead I’ll time myself and go for speedy solutions, and compare my times to the leaderboard.
